About & settings

Ready for a global workday?

Three stops, three skills: workplace expressions, idioms or jargon, and cultural awareness. Find the correct answer at each stop. A red marker means retry; green means completed. Read a phrase to the helper and collect a gift.

30 city destinations, 90 questions and 24,360 ordered destination routes. There is one question per category at each destination. A shuffled question deck for each category reduces repeats across visitors on the same browser. Routes always use different cities; two cities may be in the same country.

Shared-device progress is stored only in this browser. There are no visitor records, names, microphone recordings or analytics. If storage is blocked, the game still runs but cannot remember previous question use after reloading.

Locations are story settings, not national rules. Individual preferences matter. Map is illustrative, not to scale. Background reading: British Council: cultural behaviour and Cambridge Dictionary: touch base.


The device’s reduced-motion preference always takes priority. No sound plays.
